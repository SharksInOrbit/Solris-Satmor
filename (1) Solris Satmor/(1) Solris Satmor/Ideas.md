## Session Five
### How Solris has been affected by the death of Basil
*Solris now rejects the belief that killing is always, always wrong after watching someone that they looked up to as a mentor choose the fastest possible way to kill their attacker over a fair battle or an attempt at finding a peaceful solution.*
(Level-Up choice from The Rebel playbook)

*They have gained the **Feral** trait in response to losing Basil: a new found family member. They lost everyone as a child, but most of those losses were either a natural death, or someone choosing to move off of the longboat. With those losses they had become more quiet, nervous, and lonely. Basil's sudden, violent death reminds them of the birth family they'd forgotten: Fiercely protective wild wolves who were unapologetic with their true nature. Solris will do whatever it takes to keep those they love and avoid the pain of losing his new family after the reminder of how much it hurt to be alone after losing the protection and belonging of his birth family.*

### Downtime Plans (Two Segments)
#### Segment one
*Solris is going to explore a section of the lower hull/basement that they've been avoiding for years.
This place is where they've locked away all of the memories that have become to painful to look at. All of the abandoned belongs of the family that's left them.
They go through everything in the main cargo hold and specifically sort out any items, weapons, or information about the war, violence, and rebellion. All of those artifacts get moved into their room in exchange for the majority of blankets and soft things, which go back down into the cargo hold to create a space for the dragon to rest and a very small space in a corner of the cargo hold for themself. The rest of the cargo hold gets turned into a training room for Solris to study the blade. Other party members can use it too. The whole cargo hold is now a training space except for a small private cabin in the back of the cargo hold that Solris has been avoiding since he moved onto the boat. That cabin contains everything they have of their birth family, Solris spends some time cleaning that up and organizing it into a proper memorial for them. After the party has had a moment to grieve Basil, Solris secretly moves a few of their things down into that room as well to create a small memorial for it. They leave space in that room in case more are needed with a strong belief that the dead must be remembered.
Solris now spends the majority of their time either studying in their tower cabin, or training in the cargo hold. They still make an effort to interact with the party and occasionally they will also remember to do things like eat food as they pass through the ship between places.*
