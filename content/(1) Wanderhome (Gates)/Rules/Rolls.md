## Making Rolls
When a player wants to do something above their normal capabilities the GM may call for a roll. These actions can range from attacking to investigating their surroundings. 

When a roll is called for use 2d6 then add the total to determine the outcome of the situation. These rolls are usually made without and advantages or disadvantages.

---
## Results
**1-7:** *The action fails with consequences*
**8-10:** *the roll is a success with consequences*
**11-12:** *full success with no consequences* 
***Rolling a natural 12 will act as a natural 20.*** 

---
## Advantage and Disadvantage

- When rolling with advantage roll 3D6 and drop the lowest roll. 

- For disadvantage roll 3D6 and drop the highest roll. 

- Advantage and disadvantage can be earned in many ways such as a reward from a [[Downtime]] event or injuries making it harder to act. 

- You may also add an infinite amount of tokens up to 12 to any action roll to increase the total amount with each token adding a +1 bonus to your roll. 

- Others may use tokens to aid to your roll, but they will need two tokens to equal a +1 bonus.
