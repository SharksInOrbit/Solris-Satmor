# Downtime

Between each major arc, a period of downtime will take place. Multiple rounds of downtime may take place depending on the amount of in-game time that is being passed. 

During each round, every player will declare a goal they would like to complete during their downtime. After the goal is chosen the GM will give a DC (can be up to 20, but are usually bellow 10) and allow the player to [[Rolls|roll]] 1D6 to try to meet or beat that DC and complete their goal

---
- Small actions like educating yourself on easy to access information, building simple things, and building relationships are usually 1-6 DC. 
- Harder, more time consuming actions like finding a missing person or performing a magic ritual are usually 6-12. 
- Very laborious actions like building a house would be 12-18. 
---
After declaring your downtime action and rolling 1D6, you may also spend one token to boost the toll by +1. This can be done once per downtime round and can be spent on other players if you choose not to use it for your self. If you do not meet the DC with 1d6 and a token boost, you may subtract your total amount (roll plus token) from the DC and attempt the goal again with your new lowered DC in a future downtime round.
