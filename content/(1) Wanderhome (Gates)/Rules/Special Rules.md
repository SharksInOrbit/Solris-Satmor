## Bug Despair
The ***[[(4) Session Four (Wanderhome 5-26-24)#The Door|bug despair mechanic]]*** was one of these such rules that was only used during session four where Hira, Solris, and the Heavenblade gained bug features after failing mental health checks.

## Flashbacks
Another rule will be the ***flash back mechanic*** for session five where the players may declare a flashback to a previous event. This flashback can be anything from telling someone a bit of info or revealing that you’ve been recording the conversation the whole time. 

If the flashback is something that you would logically do without your current knowledge then you do not need to spend a token. If the action is more complex or contrived then you may need to spend anywhere between 1-5 tokens depending on how outlandish it is. For example: You flash back to tell a friend where you'll be at a meet up spot later- this wouldn't require tokens, if you flashback to setting up a huge machine that will knock someone out later then that would be 5 tokens.
