# Combat 

***Combat is rare, but not impossible in Wanderhome. Due to the nature of the world, most kith can’t comprehend the ideas of violence and combat.*** 

When a player is faced with combat against a kith that has yet to be awakened to the true nature of the world, it can be assumed that the player may easily kill or escape to avoid combat. 

When face with an awakened opponent that’s willing to fight, combat may begin. Combat is mainly dictated by the player's action rolls. At the start of the battle players may choose the order in which each player takes action instead of rolling for an initiative order.

Players are not strictly bound with what they can do in a turn, it is up to the GM to dictate when a player's turn has ended. A single turn is roughly 6 seconds and GMs will keep that in mind when deciding when a player has done enough during their turn. When a player makes an action roll in combat, enemies/environmental features may react as they also do not have a set initiative. Combatants may harm, move, or take action as consequences for players failed rolls as many times as necessary. 

**Players each start with 7 health. When your health total reaches 0 your character will die with no saving rolls.**
