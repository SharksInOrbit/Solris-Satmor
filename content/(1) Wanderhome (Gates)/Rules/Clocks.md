## Clocks

Clocks are mostly used behind the DM screen to track how long until something happens. This can be something like a guard noticing a player or a bomb going off. To start a clock the DM simply decides what it’s for and chooses a number of ticks. These act very similar to down time markers, only clocks don’t necessarily have to be ticked down by a roll. Anything can tick down a clock from a poor stealth check alerting the guard or simply time passing. The Clock may be public if the DM sees it necessary, they are simply a tool to track how soon a event is going to happen.
