## Tokens
###### ***Tokens can be any type of physical trinket, trash, or treasure.*** 

Tokens can be awarded by any party member to any other party member at any time during the session. Each party member gets to decide for themselves when to award a token and how many to give. The general guideline for token giving is to reward other member for good world building, character work, and overall great storytelling. Tokens will also be awarded by the GM for any failed rolls

Each party member should have two different collections of tokens: one bank to hold tokens they’ve spent/personally brought to the session to give and a separate collection to keep track of tokens they've earned. The number of earned tokens is retained for each player between sessions, but if a player has a lower amount of tokens than the current party average they may start out by moving up to the average level of tokens. Tokens can be spent to boost [[Rolls]], or when the game master offers you the choice to spend a token to change the outcome of a given situation.

## Token Inspiration

Remember: tokens can be any handmade item or cool piece of trash you happen to find, they don't need to be anything overcomplicated or fancy.
### Scavenged Token Ideas
- Bottle caps
- Cool/Smooth rocks
- Shells
- Tiny pinecone
- Acorns
- Cool stick
- Soda tabs
### Tokens You May Find Around The House
- Stickers
- Marbles
- Beads
- Old jewelry/charms
- Buttons
- Fake flowers
- Fun shaped paperclips
- Dice
- Tiny jar
### Crafty Tokens
- Origami
- Painted bottle caps
- Pony bead animals
- Painted marbles
- Pearler bead creations
- Bend wires/paperclips into fun shapes
- Friendship bracelets
