## Health

###### ***Players each start with 7 [[Health]]. 
###### When a player takes 4 or more points of harm in combat, they will mark their condition as Unstable.***
###### ***When your health total reaches 0 your character will die with no saving rolls.***

---
 
 The **Unstable** condition indicates a critical level of injury that will require serious medical attention. These injuries can range from simple things like a broken arm all the way to immediate concerns such as bleeding out. If a players exits combat with the **Unstable** condition, it can be assumed that they will be able to return to their base and heal the injury. If the player is unable to make it back to their base, or is unable to find a safe and well supplied alternative, they will not be able to remove the **Unstable** condition. 
 
---

Depending on the type of injury, the **Unstable** condition may cause disadvantage on many [[Rolls]]. Other players are unable to roll to heal an **Unstable** player if they do not have a special ability that will allow them to do so. There will be a cooldown for the healer in which they will not be able heal again for typically 30 minutes of in game time, but this cooldown could be longer depending on the circumstances. The injured player may be able to heal to full health during a "long rest", but they must be in a place where they have the time and supplies to treat the injury.
